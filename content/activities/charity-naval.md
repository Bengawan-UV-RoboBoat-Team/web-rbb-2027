# About the Activity
<p style="text-align: justify;">
The Naval Division of the Bengawan UV Team conducted a social visit to <b>Yayasan Pemeliharaan Anak dan Bayi Permata Hati Surakarta</b> to share care, attention, and positive interaction with children and infants.
</p>

# What We Did
<p style="text-align: justify;">
The team engaged in simple social activities, direct interaction with the children, and provided basic support as part of the visit.
</p>

# Community Engagement

This activity reflects the team’s commitment to social responsibility and community involvement beyond technical and research activities.
