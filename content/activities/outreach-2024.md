# About Robotics Class

![Outreach 2024 Documentation](/images/activities/outreach-2024-3.webp)

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Robotics is an interdisciplinary field focused on the design, development, and operation of robots. This field combines various disciplines, such as computer science, electrical engineering, materials science, and mechanics, to create mechanical systems capable of movement and interaction with their environment. With technological advancements, robotics has become one of the most dynamic and essential fields in the development of modern society.
</p>

# What We Do

![Outreach 2024 Documentation](/images/activities/outreach-2024-2.webp)

<p style="text-align: justify;">
We collaborated with the school authorities to organize a basic robotics tutorial class. The basic robotics training program at MAN 1 Surakarta is divided into three classes: manufacturing design, electronic PCB design, and programming. Students are free to choose a class according to their interests. This program is conducted over five weeks and includes both theoretical and practical sessions, concluding with an evaluation of the students' level of understanding.
</p>
