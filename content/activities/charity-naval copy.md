# About the Activity
<p style="text-align: justify;">
Bengawan UV Team organized a New Member Gathering Night for the 2025 intake, held at a villa in Tawangmangu. This overnight event was designed to build familiarity, strengthen relationships, and introduce new members to the team environment.
</p>

![Overnight Gathering new Members of Bengawan UV Team ](/images/activities/makrab2.webp)

# Activities
<p style="text-align: justify;">
The program included new member introductions and sharing sessions to exchange experiences and expectations within the team. In addition, various team-building games and group exercise activities were conducted to promote cooperation, communication, and a strong sense of togetherness among all participants.
</p>
